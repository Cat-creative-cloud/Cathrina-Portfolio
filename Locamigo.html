Digital Product Management Bootcamp

LocAmigo

Helping newcomers find familiar ingredients, suitable local alternatives and the right stores when shopping in a new country.

Role Product Manager
Duration 3 months
Focus Discovery & MVP
Output Hi-fi prototype
01

The Challenge

Grocery shopping becomes surprisingly difficult after moving abroad.

Products look different, familiar ingredients have unfamiliar names and suitable substitutes are not always obvious.

What appears to be a simple everyday task can quickly become a frustrating research exercise.

“I had my phone in my hand the entire time and had to Google every product.”
Rafael · Brazil → Switzerland
“You have no idea what you will find or how much it will cost.”
Arzu · Turkey → England
“You basically just go shopping blindly.”
Anni · Germany → England
02

Key Insight

The problem wasn't translation.

Translating an ingredient name is only one part of the journey. Newcomers also need to understand whether the product is available, which substitute will work and where to find it.

Availability

Understand whether the original ingredient is commonly available in the new country.

Alternatives

Find authentic products or suitable local substitutes.

Stores

Discover which nearby stores are likely to stock the required products.

Labels

Understand unfamiliar product names, packaging and ingredient information.

The real uncertainty starts between deciding what to cook and successfully finding the right products locally.

03

The Solution

Everything newcomers need to shop confidently.

LocAmigo combines ingredient knowledge, local store guidance and language support within one product experience.

Ingredients

Find authentic ingredients and suitable local alternatives.

Stores

Learn which nearby supermarkets and specialist shops are likely to carry the required products.

Language

Understand product names, labels and unfamiliar packaging.

05

Product Approach

We intentionally started small.

The challenge was not generating more features. It was deciding which part of the problem should be solved first.

Discover

Understand the problem

Interviews revealed where newcomers experience friction before and during grocery shopping.

Define

Focus the opportunity

Insights were translated into a focused problem statement, value proposition and MVP scope.

Build

Translate strategy into action

The concept was developed into a prototype, prioritised backlog and phased roadmap.

06

Outcome

From a broad idea to a focused product direction.

Hi-fi prototype

An interactive prototype demonstrating the core product experience.

MVP backlog

A prioritised set of user stories for the first product iteration.

Product roadmap

A Now–Next–Later roadmap covering validation and future expansion.

Business hypotheses

Initial assumptions around subscriptions, affiliates and retail partnerships.

07

Key Learning

Don’t fall in love with the solution.

The most important shift was moving from “Can we build this?” to “Does anyone need this?” The challenge was not generating ideas, but narrowing the problem enough to create a meaningful first product.
