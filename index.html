About me

I turn complex challenges into clear product direction.

I’m a problem-solver with a background in Medical Engineering, Innovation and Digital Transformation. I enjoy working at the intersection of people, data, business and technology – understanding complex challenges and turning them into better solutions.

My experience ranges from innovation management and business analysis to building digital and consumer products from discovery to delivery. What I enjoy most is bringing clarity to complex problems, connecting different perspectives and turning ideas and insights into something meaningful.

How I approach product

Understand people first. Build second.

I believe great solutions start with understanding people before building anything. I like asking the right questions, getting different perspectives and working with teams to turn complex problems into simple, meaningful solutions.

Why Product Management?

Where it all comes together.

I started my career in Medical Engineering, moved into Innovation and later Digital Transformation and Business Analysis. Along the way, I realised that I thrive when I can bring people and perspectives together – understanding users, working closely with teams and turning different ideas into solutions that solve real problems.

Product Management brings all of that together for me.
