About me

I'm a Product Manager with a background in Medical Engineering, Innovation and Digital Transformation. I enjoy working at the intersection of user needs, business strategy and technology, turning complex challenges into simple, impactful solutions.

My experience ranges from innovation management and business analysis to building digital products from discovery to delivery. What I enjoy most is bringing clarity to complex problems, aligning people around a shared direction and turning ideas into measurable value.

How I approach product

I believe great products start with understanding people before building solutions. I combine qualitative insights, data and close collaboration with cross-functional teams to make informed product decisions.

Curiosity

I ask questions before proposing solutions.

Collaboration

The best products are built together with users, designers and engineers.

Impact

I don`t only care about creating a product that actually solves a need, but delivering a great user experience. It`s not about shipping features, but about solving meaningful problems.

Why Product Management?

My path into Product Management was a natural progression. Beginning my career in Medical Engineering and Innovation before moving into Digital Transformation and Business Analysis, I became increasingly passionate about connecting technology, business strategy and user needs. Throughout this journey, I discovered that the work I enjoyed most relied on strategic thinking, communication and collaboration—qualities that come together in Product Management.
