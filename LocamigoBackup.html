Digital Product Management Bootcamp

LocAmigo

Helping newcomers find familiar ingredients, suitable local alternatives and the right stores when shopping in a new country.

Role Product Manager
Duration 3 months
Focus Discovery & MVP
Output Hi-fi prototype
01

The Challenge

Grocery shopping becomes surprisingly difficult after moving abroad.

Familiar ingredients have unfamiliar names, products look different and suitable substitutes are not always obvious.

“I had my phone in my hand the entire time and had to Google every product.”
Rafael · Brazil → Switzerland
“You have no idea what you will find or how much it will cost.”
Arzu · Turkey → England
“You basically just go shopping blindly.”
Anni · Germany → England
02

Research & Insight

The real challenge was not translation. It was making confident choices.

Interviews with three people who had moved abroad revealed the same pattern: newcomers need to know what a product is, whether they can find it, what they can use instead and where to shop.

Availability

Can I find the product locally?

Alternatives

What can I use instead?

Stores

Where am I most likely to find it?

03

The MVP

Start small: one cuisine, one market, one clear problem.

Brazil → Switzerland. Users manually enter a familiar product, LocAmigo checks whether it is available and suggests suitable local alternatives and stores where it can be found.

Manual input · No live inventory
