Process automation · Volunteer project

Activity Sign-up System

Replacing a fragmented, manual registration process with a lightweight web application for activity sign-ups, calendar invitations and payment tracking.

Try the live app ↗
Role Volunteer & Product Builder
Context Language school in Spain
Focus Workflow Optimization
Tools Google Sheets, Apps Script, HTML, CSS
01

The context

A small administrative task had become a repetitive workflow.

During my volunteer placement at a language school in Spain, I was responsible for coordinating extracurricular activities for international students.

Updating the programme initially sounded straightforward. In practice, registrations, payments and participant communication were spread across several disconnected documents and manual steps.

02

The previous process

Every change triggered a chain of manual updates.

The weekly activity schedule was maintained in Word, printed and displayed at the school. Registrations and payments then had to be checked and tracked separately.

01

Update Word document

Activities, dates and available places were edited manually.

02

Print and display

The latest version had to be printed and placed on the notice board.

03

Track registrations

Student sign-ups were collected and maintained separately.

04

Check payments

Payment status was compared against another list.

05

Follow up manually

Students with outstanding payments had to be identified and reminded.

03

The initiative

I kept thinking: this must be easier.

I first explored whether an existing tool could cover the school's requirements without introducing unnecessary complexity or recurring costs.

When I could not find a sufficiently simple and cost-effective solution, I decided to create a lightweight web application myself.

Instead of adapting the school's workflow to a complex tool, I built a simple tool around the workflow the team already understood.
04

The solution

One Google Sheet became the operational source of truth.

Activities are maintained in Google Sheets. The application uses this information to display available activities and automatically processes each registration.

01

Activity management

The team updates activities directly in Google Sheets instead of recreating and printing a new Word document.

02

Automatic registration

New registrations are automatically added to a dedicated participant tab.

03

Confirmation and calendar

Participants receive a confirmation together with Google Calendar and ICS options.

04

Payment tracking

Payment status remains visible in the operational list and can be updated manually by the organiser.

05

Conditional reminders

A payment reminder is only sent when the participant has not yet been marked as paid.

05

The outcome

A simpler process for both the school and the students.

1 central operational overview
0 printed registration sheets required
Automatic confirmations and calendar invitations
Conditional payment follow-ups instead of blanket reminders

Reflection

Good solutions do not always need to be large.

This project reinforced that operational improvements often come from understanding the complete workflow rather than digitising an isolated step. A lightweight solution tailored to the team's actual needs created more value than introducing a more complex external platform.
